# Chart 7 — Teen AI Companion Use

Editorial-quality stats for chart annotations and prose.

**Source:** Common Sense Media, "Talk, Trust, and Trade-Offs: How and Why Teens Use AI Companions" (July 16, 2025). Fielded April–May 2025 by NORC at the University of Chicago. Nationally representative sample of n=1,060 US teens 13–17.

## Headline editorial line

> Among American teens who use AI companions, one in three has chosen the AI over a real person for serious conversations.

**Source:** Q9. *"Have you ever chosen to talk to an AI companion instead of a real person about something important or serious?"*
- Yes (NET): **33%** of AI-companion-using teens (n=758)
- Across all US teens 13–17: roughly 33% × 72% ≈ **24%** (about 1 in 4)
- Margin of error: ±3.4pp at 95% confidence

## Methodology footnote

Survey fielded online, April–May 2025, via NORC's AmeriSpeak probability-based panel. Weighted to Census benchmarks. Cited in the chart as: *"Common Sense Media (2025), 'Talk, Trust, and Trade-Offs.' Survey of US teens 13–17, n=1,060, fielded April–May 2025 by NORC at the University of Chicago."*
